# Project-management task distribution

Prepared 2026-10-20 from the SE3003 Semester Project Brief and official M1 template.
This is a work allocation, not a claim of completed work. M1 is the immediate priority; later milestones remain planned. Confirm deadlines in the LMS.

## Shared submission and individual evidence

Prepare one shared report per milestone using its official template. Each member works from their own GitHub account and links their actual commits, pull requests, reviews and other evidence. Assignment alone does not establish contribution. A draft prepared with assistance must be reviewed and verified before it is presented as completed work.

For each issue: start a branch, prepare the listed output, open a linked pull request, obtain another member's review, and move the issue to Done only after acceptance. Upload completed outputs in separate meaningful commits/PRs, rather than artificially splitting files to increase contribution counts. Repository publication does not replace LMS submission.

## Files, tables, charts and presentation

- `docs/m1/` through `docs/m5/`: working sections and official milestone reports (editable DOCX plus reviewed PDF). Tables required by M1 belong in that shared report.
- `docs/m2/estimates.xlsx`: effort estimates and assumptions; `docs/m2/schedule.xlsx`: dependencies, dates, owners and the release/sprint plan or Gantt. These are planned files, not existing deliverables.
- `docs/metrics/progress.xlsx`: dated source data for planned vs actual scope/effort, completed work and defects. Generate charts from real measurements; record units, definitions, date and evidence links. No invented history or fabricated progress.
- `docs/m5/presentation.pptx`: final showcase with problem/value, approach, scope/trade-offs, planned vs actual, a risk/change, quality evidence and lessons. Export a readable PDF when useful. The brief requires the final management presentation; it does not establish a separate slide deck for every milestone.
- `docs/weekly/YYYY-Www.md`: team, week, milestone, completed work, next work, top blocker/risk, planned vs actual and reasons, decisions/support, and each member's real evidence links.
- `docs/decisions/` and `docs/quality/`: supporting decision and test evidence.

Keep source data editable and export charts as readable images for reports/slides. Caption each chart or screenshot with what it proves. Retrieve the official M2-M5 templates when available before final assembly. Peer evaluations follow the private course submission process.
